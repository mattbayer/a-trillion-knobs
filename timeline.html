Timeline

The breakthroughs in order. Each links to the chapter where it is discussed.

Foundations and the first winter

1943 McCulloch and Pitts show networks of threshold units can compute any logical function. [ch 1]

1948 Shannon founds information theory, and builds the first n-gram language models. [ch 5]

1958 Rosenblatt's perceptron: a neuron that learns its weights from examples. [ch 1]

1959 Hubel and Wiesel find edge-detecting cells in the cat visual cortex. [ch 3]

1969 Minsky and Papert's Perceptrons: a single layer cannot learn XOR. Funding dries up. [ch 1]

Backprop and the second winter

1982 Hopfield networks: memory as energy minimisation. (Nobel Prize in Physics, 2024.) [ch 13]

1986 Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams popularise backpropagation; hidden layers learn representations. [ch 2]

1989 Universal approximation theorem. LeCun's convolutional network reads zip codes. [ch 3]

1991 Hochreiter identifies the vanishing gradient problem. [ch 3]

1997 LSTM (Hochreiter and Schmidhuber). Deep Blue beats Kasparov by search. [ch 6, ch 9]

1998 LeNet-5 reads cheques commercially. Support vector machines dominate research. [ch 3]

2003 Bengio's neural language model: learned word embeddings. [ch 5]

2006 Hinton's deep belief networks; the "deep learning" rebrand. [ch 3]

The deep learning revolution

2007 Nvidia releases CUDA: GPUs for general computing. [ch 4]

2009 ImageNet released by Fei-Fei Li. [ch 4]

2012 AlexNet wins ImageNet by ten points. The auction at Lake Tahoe. [ch 4]

2013 word2vec: king − man + woman ≈ queen. DeepMind's Atari player. [ch 5, ch 11]

2014 Sequence-to-sequence learning; attention (Bahdanau); Adam; GANs. [ch 6]

2015 Batch normalisation; ResNet (152 layers). Diffusion models proposed. OpenAI founded. [ch 4, ch 10]

2016 AlphaGo beats Lee Sedol. Google Translate switches to neural networks. [ch 11, ch 6]

The transformer era

2017 "Attention Is All You Need". AlphaGo Zero learns from self-play alone. [ch 7, ch 11]

2018 GPT-1 and BERT: pre-train, then fine-tune. [ch 8]

2019 GPT-2 (1.5 billion weights). Sutton's "The Bitter Lesson". [ch 8, ch 9]

2020 "Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models". GPT-3 (175 billion weights). Vision Transformer. AlphaFold 2. Modern diffusion models. [ch 9, ch 8, ch 7, ch 10]

2021 CLIP, DALL-E. Anthropic founded. Codex and GitHub Copilot. [ch 10]

2022 Chinchilla; chain-of-thought prompting; Stable Diffusion; toy models of superposition; ChatGPT (30 November). US chip export controls. [ch 9, ch 11, ch 10, ch 12]

Assistants, reasoning and agents

2023 GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Llama. Hinton leaves Google to warn about risk. Sparse autoencoders find interpretable features. [ch 10, ch 12]

2024 Million-token context windows. Golden Gate Claude. o1, the first reasoning model. Nobel Prizes in Physics (Hopfield, Hinton) and Chemistry (Hassabis, Jumper, Baker). [ch 10, ch 12, ch 11, ch 13]

2025 DeepSeek R1; reasoning models everywhere. Coding agents become practical. METR time-horizon trend. Circuit tracing in production models. Models reach International Mathematical Olympiad gold-medal standard. Data-centre plans measured in gigawatts. [ch 11, ch 12, ch 9]
