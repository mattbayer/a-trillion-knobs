Interactive demos

Eight small things to play with after listening. Each runs entirely in this page, with no internet connection, and each ends with a short list of experiments worth trying.

A single neuronCh. 1 · Turn the weights and bias by hand. Run Rosenblatt's rule. Fail at XOR. Gradient descentCh. 2 · Roll downhill on a loss surface. Learning rate, momentum, noise. A tiny network, learningCh. 3–4 · Backprop live. Watch hidden layers un-crumple the data. Convolution kernelsCh. 3 · One neuron slid across an image. Edit the nine weights. Shannon's language modelCh. 5 · Next-character prediction by counting, trained on this book. Softmax and temperatureCh. 5 · Logits to probabilities. The Boltzmann distribution in disguise. Attention, by handCh. 6–7 · Drag queries and keys. See the weights and the blend. Scaling lawsCh. 9, 11 · Power laws on linear vs log axes. Compounding reliability.
