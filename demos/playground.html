Things to try

  • Rings, as loaded. Watch the right-hand panel: the network pulls the inner disc and the outer ring apart until a straight line separates them. That is the "un-crumpling" from chapter 3. The hidden layers do not solve the problem; they change coordinates until it is easy.
  • Set hidden layers to 0. You are back to a single neuron: one straight cut, hopeless on rings and XOR.
  • Restore the layers and set the activation to Linear. However many layers you add, it behaves like zero layers. A stack of linear maps is one linear map; the bend is what makes depth mean something.
  • Spiral is hard. Try 1 layer of 10, then 3 layers of 8. Depth buys expressiveness more cheaply than width.
  • Count the weights (shown below the controls). A few hundred knobs, set entirely by gradient descent, starting from random.
