Softmax and temperature

Chapter 5. The network's last layer gives a raw score (a logit) for every token. Softmax turns scores into probabilities: pᵢ = exp(zᵢ/T) / Σ exp(zⱼ/T). It is the Boltzmann distribution with the logits as negative energies.

Things to try

  • Lower T toward 0. The distribution freezes into its ground state: the model always says "mat". This is "greedy" decoding: deterministic and repetitive.
  • Raise T to 2 or 3. Everything becomes nearly equally likely and "parliament" starts appearing. High temperature is more creative, and then incoherent.
  • Watch the entropy: the model's uncertainty in bits. And the loss if the true word was "sofa": that is the cross-entropy loss, −log p(correct). Raise the "sofa" logit and watch the loss fall. Training is nothing more than nudging weights so that this number, averaged over trillions of tokens, goes down.
  • Only differences between logits matter: adding a constant to all of them changes nothing, just as the zero of energy is arbitrary.
