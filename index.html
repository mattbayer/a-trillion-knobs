How to use this

Listen first. Each chapter is written for the ear and stands on its own without figures. Afterwards, open the chapter's page here: below the text you will find the maths written out, a demo or two, and links to the primary sources. The MP3 files are in site/audio if you want to copy them to a phone, and the whole book is in output as an EPUB.
